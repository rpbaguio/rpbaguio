### `$ ls ~/schoolees-suite`

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| **SchooleesCore** | Campus management for the registrar, deans, faculty, cashier and administrators: admissions, curriculum, grading, registrar services and cashiering. |
| **SchooleesPortal** | The student portal: applications, courses, study load, grades, exam permits, statements and requests. |
| **SchooleesApp** | SchooleesPortal's companion on Android and iOS: an NFC-readable digital ID, attendance taps, invoices and permits. |
| **SchooleesPay** | Online payment of school fees through Bux or Maya, posted by the cashier so every peso is invoiced. |
| **SchooleesPeople** | HR, timekeeping, leave and payroll: the 201 file, self-service, pay runs, 13th month and government remittances. |
| **SchooleesPulse** | A Windows attendance kiosk and ID station: NFC/RFID taps that work offline, a staff time clock, and ID card design and printing. |
| **Jarvees AI** | The campus assistant for staff and students, answering from the school's own documents. |
